# Eramba GRC Cybersecurity Compliance Project

## Overview

A Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) project implemented using the Eramba platform for a simulated government digital services organization.

The project focused on integrating cybersecurity risk management, policies, internal controls, compliance requirements, data privacy, third-party risks, and security incidents within a centralized GRC platform.

- Policy versions and reviews

- Linking policies and controls to identified risks

## Policies and Internal Controls

As part of my role, I worked on the policy and internal control components of the Eramba implementation.

The project included four security policies and four internal controls.

### Policies

The implemented policies covered areas such as:

- Identity Governance
- Data Resilience
- Email Security
- Incident Response

### Internal Controls

The implemented controls included:

- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Backup and Recovery
- Email Filtering
- Incident Reporting

The controls were connected with relevant policies and risks to demonstrate the relationship between governance requirements and cybersecurity risk management.
